# 更新日志 Change log

## [1.0.0]

### 特性 Feature

- 玩家摔落到泥巴方块减免80%的伤害
- 根据摔落高度不同概率变化为可疑沙砾或可疑沙子
- 所有概率和高度参数可在配置文件中更改

- Players who fall into mud block will take 80% less damage
- According to the different probability of falling height, mud block changes to suspicious sand or suspicious sand
- All probability and height parameters can be changed in the configuration file


## [1.0.1]

### 新增 New

- 玩家摔落出砖或者触发胡萝卜彩蛋时，默认受到2点真实伤害（被砖磕到了=_=）
- 在下雨天或者雷雨天，触发胡萝卜彩蛋的概率增加
- 优化了战利品表，现在在下界或者末地转化可疑方块会使用不同的战利品表
- When players drop and get a brick or trigger carrot easter egg, they will take 2 points of void damage by default -- got hit by the brick.
- During rain or thunderstorms, the chance of triggering the carrot easter egg is increased.
- Optimized the loot tables: now converting suspicious blocks in the Nether or the End will use different loot tables.
